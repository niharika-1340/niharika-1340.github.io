About me

[Draft — replace with your own voice.] I got into robotics through the mechanical side first: servos, calibration jigs, the parts of a humanoid that actually have to move without grinding themselves apart. Three years at Hanson Robotics in Hong Kong taught me that a robot's personality means nothing if the hardware underneath it can't be trusted.

That's the thread running through my research at CMU now — manipulation and teleoperation, and specifically how a robot should behave differently once a person is in the loop, physically close, or asking for something it wasn't explicitly trained to do.

I did my BEng in Mechanical Engineering at City University of Hong Kong, and I'm now finishing an MS in Mechanical Engineering — Research at Carnegie Mellon, in the RCHI Lab, advised by Zackory Erickson and Aviral Kumar.
