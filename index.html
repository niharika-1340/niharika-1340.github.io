I build robots that can
work alongside people.

I'm a graduate researcher at Carnegie Mellon, working on manipulation and teleoperation — the layer of a robot's behavior that decides how it moves when a person is close by, or asking it to do something new.

I'm an MS student in Mechanical Engineering — Research at CMU, in the RCHI Lab, advised by Zackory Erickson and Aviral Kumar. Before this I spent three years at Hanson Robotics in Hong Kong building and calibrating humanoid hardware, which is a large part of why I care about robots that hold up outside a lab.
